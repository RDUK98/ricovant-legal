# RICOVANT Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 18 October 2026**

RICOVANT respects the privacy of users of RICOVANT Guard for Rovo.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the RICOVANT Guard for Rovo application within Atlassian products.

## 1. Information we process

RICOVANT Guard may process information necessary to evaluate and govern actions initiated through Atlassian Rovo and Forge.

Depending on how the application is used, this may include:

- action or request details submitted to RICOVANT Guard;
- technical information required to process and verify an action;
- security and policy evaluation results;
- timestamps, action identifiers and audit information;
- limited Atlassian account, site or installation identifiers where required for operation of the application.

RICOVANT does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless it is included by a user in information submitted to the application.

## 2. How information is used

Information is processed only where necessary to:

- evaluate proposed AI or automated actions;
- apply security and governance policies;
- return ALLOW, HUMAN REVIEW or BLOCK decisions;
- support approval and verification workflows;
- prevent duplicate or replayed actions;
- maintain security, reliability and audit records;
- troubleshoot and improve the service.

## 3. Data sharing

RICOVANT does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers or infrastructure providers where necessary to operate RICOVANT Guard.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

## 4. Data retention

RICOVANT retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain appropriate security and audit records, or satisfy legal obligations.

Where information is no longer required, it will be deleted or anonymised where reasonably practicable.

## 5. Security

RICOVANT uses reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Atlassian

RICOVANT Guard operates in connection with Atlassian products.

Atlassian's own processing of information is governed by Atlassian's applicable privacy policies and terms.

## 7. International processing

Information may be processed in jurisdictions where RICOVANT's infrastructure or service providers operate, subject to applicable data-protection requirements.
